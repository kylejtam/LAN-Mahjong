**A big update: shuffling at the table, two-tap claims and a tutorial**

At the start of every hand
- No more Start / Ready pop-up: you see the jumbled tiles at the table and pick a seat right there.
- Push the tiles around with your mouse or finger to shuffle. Everyone sees the same pile, and the computer players shuffle too.
- Press Ready (or Unready if you change your mind). When all four are ready, the tiles get a few final shoves and the wall is built.
- The dice stay inside the wall. The dealer takes the 1st and 5th tiles together, like at a real table, and starts with all 14 tiles sorted.
- Opening flowers pop up in front of each player: green if they score for that player, red if not.
- The leftover wall shrinks smoothly into the small tiles-left box.

During the hand
- Claiming is now two taps: the first holds the tile (nobody can draw it), then Confirm or Cancel.
- New setting: Call all possible claims holds every tile you could claim for you.
- Undo now takes back your own last move.
- Flowers always sit on each player's left, and new flowers glow green or red.
- Your tiles stay the same size all hand.
- New fullscreen button (not on iPhone).
- If someone loses their connection, the game pauses for everyone until they're back, and it's much clearer who it is.

After the hand
- Remaining tiles button in the game summary shows what was left in the wall.
- After the summary you go back to the table, with Return to Game Summary in the middle.
- After the last hand, you go straight to the final scores.

New: a Tutorial at the top of the game list walks you through every control with 3 computer players.
