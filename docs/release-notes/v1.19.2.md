**Smaller fixes and polish**

- The window-breaking sound now plays only once per player per hand: when they first draw a flower in play that doesn't score for them. It's decided by the game server, so every device hears it at the same moment.
- Sound on iPads: fixed for iPads that pause sound after the screen locks. The first tap now wakes it.
- The full screen icon is centred in its button.
- The shuffling pile now moves the way each player sees it: if East pushes up, South sees the tiles move from left to right.
- Empty seats show the whole name tag as a blue, pulsing Sit here button.
- If you left your seat after a hand, you can close the game summary to see the table, and sit down again.
- Remaining tiles marks where the draw starts.
- The game's name shows to the left of the round number in the top bar.
