**Tutorial polish, a window-breaking sound, and Close host**

Tutorial
- Nothing moves on by itself: every step waits for you to do what it asks, or to press Next.
- Whatever you need to press glows gold, and the rest of the screen is dimmed except a circle around it.
- Clearer steps: closing the help and settings, "Everyone is ready", reading about your flowers.
- On a discard step, only the right tile can be pushed out of your hand.

In the game
- When you hold a claim, only the tiles used in it glow (not your whole hand).
- The settings are in a clearer order: name, language, win celebration, Call all possible claims.
- The first time in a hand that someone draws a flower that doesn't score for them, you hear a window breaking.

Host page
- New Close host button next to the version number. Click it twice to close LAN Mahjong (your games are saved).
