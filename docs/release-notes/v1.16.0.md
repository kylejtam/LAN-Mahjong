**The end of the wall**
- After the last tile is thrown, the game waits for anyone who can win with it. There's no time limit, and a new plain **Pass** button says no.
- Anyone who can chow, pong or gong gets 10 seconds to decide.
- Only when nobody can take the tile does the hand end. A red seal stamp shows **Tie** (和) for 5 seconds, then the summary opens.
- Winning on that last discard is now called **河底撈魚 Win on the last discard** (1 fan).

**Winning**
- A red **Wu!** bubble pops up beside the winner's name.
- The winner's whole hand shows in the middle of the table, with the winning tile glowing gold, while the confetti plays.
- The summary opens 10 seconds later.

**CPUs**
- A CPU that can win or pong a tile now always gets to, even if the next player would otherwise draw first.
- New games give CPUs 5 seconds per move (change it on the host page).
