**A new start to every hand**
- The tiles are shoved around face down, then glide straight into a square wall, all four sides at once.
- The dealer gets a **Roll the dice** button in the middle of the wall (swiping the dice still works).
- After the dice, a light counts round the walls and along the stacks, and a gold mark shows where the wall breaks.
- The tiles are dealt from the wall in the real order, then the wall shrinks into the small "tiles left" box and play begins.
