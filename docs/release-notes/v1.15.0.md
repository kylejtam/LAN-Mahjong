**New CPU players.** After many thousands of test games, Easy, Medium and Hard have been rebuilt:

- **Easy** plays like a natural beginner: it never calls chow, pong or gong, and throws its loneliest tile first.
- **Medium** follows a simple plan (one suit, or a concealed hand), but is slow to claim tiles.
- **Hard** plans its hand for big wins: one suit, all pongs, dragons and winds, pure one suit. It avoids feeding players who show a big hand.

At every minimum fan, Hard now wins the most chips, then Medium, then Easy.
