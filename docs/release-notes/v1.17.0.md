**Easier to play**
- **Drawing:** just touch the glowing tile in the middle to draw it.
- **Discarding:** push a tile up out of your hand. When it glows red, let go to throw it. Let go earlier and it goes back.
- **Choosing a Soeng:** when more than one is possible, the tiles you can use glow in your hand. Tap the ones you want; no pop-up box.
- **Whose turn:** the player's name tag pulses gold and the table edge in front of them glows.
- **After a win or a tie:** tap anywhere to go straight to the summary.
- **Win summary:** the winner's name is green and the name of the player who threw the winning tile is red.

**Host page**
- Each game is now a tile. Tap it to open its seats, history and settings. A new game opens by itself.
- Tap any chip win or loss in the history to change it. Only the difference changes that player's chips, so chips you typed in by hand are kept. Edits are marked in orange.
